PANDA v1.0.64

Powder Analysis for Neutron Diffraction Applications

Browse, process and visualise neutron powder-diffraction data straight from the instrument: open a data folder, pick the runs, and go from raw NeXus files to normalised, background-subtracted patterns ready for Rietveld refinement.

  • Normalise, bin, combine and subtract backgrounds (Chebyshev, SNIP, arPLS…)
  • 1D, 2D heat-map and 3D views of temperature or pressure series
  • 2D-detector integration with statistical uncertainties
  • Peak fitting and export to .xys
  • Built-in pyPRF viewer for FullProf Rietveld results

Edit_PyCR v0.3.0

FullProf PCR editor

A dedicated editor for FullProf .pcr refinement files. It understands the file — which numbers are refinement codes, where each phase and pattern begins, what each keyword means — so less time goes into counting columns and more into refining.

  • Syntax highlighting, folding and a structure outline of phases and patterns
  • Guided dialogs for cell, atoms, profile, background and excluded regions
  • CIF / mCIF → PCR conversion and templates, using FullProf’s own programs
  • Sequential refinements and staged refinement strategies
  • Run FullProf, plot the .prf pattern and follow χ² and R-factors

About

Background

My research uses neutron and X-ray diffraction to connect crystal and magnetic structure with physical properties. A large part of it concerns molecule-based and hybrid materials — coordination polymers, metal–organic frameworks and hybrid perovskites — where subtle structural changes such as order–disorder transitions, incommensurate modulations or rearrangements of the hydrogen-bond network switch on ferroelectricity, magnetoelectric coupling or proton conduction.

The short wavelength of D9 makes it ideal for accurate structural work: hydrogen positions in minerals and molecular crystals, anharmonic thermal motion, and data to very high momentum transfer. Recent studies include incommensurate magnetic order in formate perovskites, spin spirals in layered perovskites and Weyl semimetals, lattice dynamics in superionic conductors, and crystals followed across wide temperature and pressure ranges.

A physicist by training (PhD, Universidad de La Laguna, 2008), I was instrument scientist on the CRG powder diffractometer D1B for ICMA (CSIC – Universidad de Zaragoza) before joining the ILL Diffraction Group in 2014.

Research

What I work on

Single-crystal neutron diffraction

High-resolution data to large momentum transfer: precise atomic positions, hydrogen bonding, thermal motion and disorder.

Magnetic structures & multiferroics

Commensurate and incommensurate magnetic order, spin densities and magnetoelectric coupling, with polarised and unpolarised neutrons.

Molecular magnets & MOFs

Coordination polymers, metal–organic frameworks and hybrid perovskites: how the linker and the lattice shape the magnetism.

Phase transitions under extreme conditions

Thermal expansion, high pressure and structural phase transitions followed with neutron and X-ray diffraction.

Instruments

Where the data come from

Responsible

D9 Hot neutron four-circle diffractometer

One of the shortest neutron wavelengths available at any reactor (0.35 – 0.85 Å), a Eulerian cradle and a 2D detector: Bragg intensities to very high momentum transfer, atomic displacements resolved to ~0.001 Å, and access to absorbing elements such as Gd or Sm.
